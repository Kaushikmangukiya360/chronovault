chronovault is designed to support compliance programs by improving data-at-rest protections and evidencing activity.

## GDPR

- Tenant isolation reduces cross-customer exposure.
- Delete semantics can support right-to-erasure workflows.
- Audit exports provide accountability records.

## HIPAA

- Encrypted persistence supports confidentiality requirements.
- Scoped access tokens and IP allowlists reduce unauthorized access.
- Immutable audit chains support access traceability.

## SOC2

- Security controls are explicit in storage, auth, and audit systems.
- Tamper detection and lock-protected writes support integrity objectives.
